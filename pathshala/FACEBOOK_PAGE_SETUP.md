## 3. Monetization: what to aim for

Meta changes these programs often, so check **Professional Dashboard → Monetization** (or the Meta Creator page) for the current rules. As of my knowledge:

- Facebook combined in-stream ads, reels ads and the Performance bonus into one program, **Facebook Content Monetization**. It started as invite-only and is opening up. You can join a waitlist from the Professional Dashboard.
- **General requirements:** you're 18+, you're in an eligible country (India is eligible), the page follows Meta's **Partner Monetization Policies** and **Content Monetization Policies**, and the content is **original**.
- **Older in-stream ads rules** (useful as a target): 5,000 followers and 60,000 minutes watched in the last 60 days. Reels count as watch time now, so posting daily is the fastest way to get there.
- **Other income that opens up earlier:** Stars (fans tip you during reels and lives), Subscriptions (usually needs about 10,000 followers), and brand deals (laptops, ed-tech courses, coding platforms).

### What keeps the page monetizable

- **100% original content:** your own script, your own voice, your own editing (the EP01 video meets this). Don't repost other creators' clips, and don't use copyrighted songs in reels you want to monetize. The EP01 track is original, so it's safe.
- **No "engagement bait":** don't say "Like karo agar…" or "Share karo warna…". Asking a genuine question ("Comment mein batao: …") is fine.
- **Be consistent:** post one reel every day at the same time. Good slots for Indian students are 7–9 PM IST, or 1 PM on weekends.
- **Watch time matters most:** keep reels 20–30 s, put a strong hook in the first 1 s, and end with a loop (the end card cuts back to the hook).
- **Upload natively:** upload the MP4 to Facebook directly (don't share a YouTube link) and turn on the AI-generated **captions/subtitles**.
- Reply to comments in the first hour, and pin a comment with the next episode's topic.
