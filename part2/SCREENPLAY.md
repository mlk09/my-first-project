## 1. Story concept

Picking up right after Part 1's wink, Bolt and Boo have the biggest candy pile in the forest. At midnight it vanishes, candy by candy, and **glowing three-toed footprints** lead through the graveyard to the haunted house on the hill. Inside, a giant clawed shadow rises on the wall. It belongs to **Granny Gloom**, a tiny old ghost whose "claws" are knitting needles. She did take the candy, but only for the **Wisplings**, five baby ghosts nobody shares with because they look "too spooky". Boo remembers being left out. Bolt has an idea they can't manage alone: **share with everyone, together**. The forest's jack-o'-lanterns are scared of the Wisplings until the smallest one offers them a candy, and then everyone celebrates.

**Lesson:** share, be kind, and don't judge others by how they look.

**Twist for Part 3:** Bolt realises *"Granny, you float. Ghosts don't have FEET!"* So who made the footprints? They lead into the dark forest, where two huge eyes open… *CRUNCH*.

## 4. Blender scene and animation directions

These are the steps `scene_p2.py` follows. Use them to rebuild or extend the episode in your own Blender project.

**Reused from Part 1 (`edit3/scene3d.py`):** the forest, ground, path, graveyard, pumpkins, haunted house, moon, fog, fireflies, Bolt, Boo, candy and hearts. Fog is a node group mixing every material towards the fog colour by camera distance; the jack-o'-lantern faces, eyes and windows are driven through object colour.

**New assets:**
- **Footprints:** one mesh (a flattened sphere pad plus three toe ovals) instanced every 0.55 m, alternating left and right of a polyline. It uses an emissive material set through object colour (cyan-green), lit up in sequence (0.1 s apart).
- **Granny Gloom:** a lathe-revolved sheet profile with a wavy hem; torus spectacles; a tube shawl and nightcap; cylinder needles on an empty that rocks for the knitting loop.
- **Wisplings:** the same lathe at 0.25 m with pastel colours, instanced five times.
- **Interior:** an 8×6×3.3 m box room built far from the forest (x = 150). It has a fireplace with flickering emission and a point light, a window with a moon disc, curtains, a table with a 170-candy cone pile, a rocking chair, portraits and three candles with flickering lights.
- **Wall shadow:** Granny's outline extruded flat and enlarged, with needle "claws" and a cap "horn". It uses a black emission/transparency mix, fades in and scales up from 1:12.
- **Door:** a hinge empty that rotates −92° over 2.2 s.
- **Claw branch:** tapered tubes with bony fingers.
- **Mystery eyes:** two emissive ellipsoids whose Z scale animates for the blink.

**Animation:** everything is a function of time inside a frame-change handler:
- Mouths flap from the voice-volume envelopes (`work/sync.json`).
- Bolt's eyes, arms and "?/!" pop-ups run from key tracks with easing (smoothstep, ease-out, overshoot).

**Lighting:**
- Outside: moonlight sun (cool blue), a lilac rim, warm pumpkin point lights, fog density 0.035.
- Inside: the sun is dimmed to 0.05; candles, fireplace and the window light take over; fog is lowered to 0.012.

**Cameras:** one camera per shot with a pushing, orbiting or tracking position and target, plus depth of field, handheld wobble and impact shakes.

**Render:** Eevee at 1280×536 with 5 samples, upscaled in post. On your RTX 5050, use `--samples 16 --shadows --pct 150` for native 1920×804 with real shadows.

**Asset reuse ideas for Part 3:**
- The footprint instancer can lay any trail.
- The lathe ghost can become any new ghost by changing its profile and colour.
- The room box can become any interior.

## 8. Part 3 teaser: "The Footprints in the Dark"

The huge yellow eyes belong to **Crumbleclaw**, a giant, shy, fuzzy forest creature with three-toed feet. It has been *returning* candy that the wind blew away, and crunches loudly only because it's nervous. Bolt, Boo, Granny and the Wisplings head into the deep forest to find it, and learn that the biggest, loudest friend can be the gentlest. Final gag: Crumbleclaw sneezes, and every candy in the forest rains down on Bolt's head.
